# Analytics Report for Superstore, USA

## Project Overview
This interactive Tableau dashboard provides a comprehensive sales and profitability analysis for a US-based superstore business. The dashboard is designed to help stakeholders monitor key business metrics, identify regional sales performance, evaluate product category trends, and analyze customer segments for better decision-making.

---

## Objective
The primary objective of this dashboard is to:
- Analyze overall sales performance
- Track profit margins and return rates
- Identify high-performing states and product categories
- Understand customer segment contribution
- Monitor monthly sales trends
- Discover top-selling products

---

## Tools Used
- Tableau
- Excel / CSV Dataset

---

## Key KPIs
- **Total Sales:** 2.297M
- **Profit Margin:** 12.47%
- **Return Rate:** 5.91%

---

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales by Segments
Displays contribution of different customer segments:
- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

### 2. Category by Sales
Analyzes sales distribution across:
- Technology
- Furniture
- Office Supplies

### 3. Sales by States
Geographical visualization showing state-wise sales performance across the USA.

### 4. Sales by Sub-Category
Highlights top-performing product sub-categories such as:
- Phones
- Chairs
- Storage
- Tables

### 5. Monthly Sales Trend
Tracks monthly sales fluctuations and overall growth trend throughout the year.

### 6. Top 5 Products by Sales
Displays highest revenue-generating products.

---

## Filters Included
Interactive filters allow users to analyze data dynamically by:
- Metrics
- Year of Order Date
- Region

---

## Key Insights
- Consumer segment contributes the highest share of sales.
- Technology category generates the highest revenue among all categories.
- California records the highest sales among states.
- Phones and Chairs are leading sub-categories by sales.
- Sales show strong growth toward the final months of the year.
